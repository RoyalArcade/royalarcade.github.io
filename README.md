## ⚡ Game Features
* **60-Second Blitz:** Race against the clock before time expires.
* **Hydra Trajectory Physics:** Burst 1 bird to trigger 3 dynamic child birds carrying momentum across the sky.
* **Flow-State Tuning:** Missing a shot startles a bird away and softens flight speed so you can recover your rhythm.
* **Harmonic Chiptunes:** Musical hit cascades synthesized in real-time via Web Audio API (no heavy MP3 downloads).
* **3 Difficulty Tiers:** Chill, Pro, and Chaos modes.
* **Saved High Scores:** Tracks your personal best score directly in local browser storage.
* **Instagram Ready:** Built in a vertical 9:16 mobile aspect ratio with one-tap score sharing to Instagram Stories.

---

## 📱 How to Play on Mobile / Instagram
1. Tap on the game link or sticker in an Instagram Story.
2. Tap moving birds on your screen to score points and build combos.
3. Keep your accuracy high to boost flock speed and climb the ranks!
